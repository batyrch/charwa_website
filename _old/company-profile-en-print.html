About Us

Charwa GmbH is a Berlin-based B2B trading company specializing in used commercial vehicles. We source premium trucks and semi-trailers from established suppliers within the European Union and sell them to business customers. We combine deep automotive industry expertise with proprietary technology to deliver a fast, transparent, and reliable trading experience.

Operating with a lean, efficient structure, Charwa is led by a hands-on Managing Director supported by a network of specialized consultants — bringing together automotive expertise, cutting-edge technology, and strong relationships with trusted vehicle suppliers across the EU.

How We Trade

Charwa operates a straightforward B2B trading model built on trust, transparency, and efficiency:

Established EU Suppliers — We source exclusively from verified dealers and fleet operators across the European Union, ensuring vehicle quality and documentation integrity.

Business Customers — Our buyers are registered businesses. Every transaction is conducted on a company-to-company basis with proper commercial documentation.

Bank Transfer Payments — All payments are processed via standard bank transfers with invoice references — no cash transactions, full audit trail.

How We Work

1
Sourcing — Proprietary systems scan 13 European marketplaces around the clock. Best matches are delivered instantly via automated alerts.
2
Evaluation & Purchase — Each vehicle is vetted against customer requirements. We handle price negotiations and coordinate inspections with our established EU suppliers.
3
Documentation — Semi-automated systems generate invoices, sales contracts, and commercial documents — reducing errors and turnaround time to minutes.
4
Payment & Settlement — All transactions are settled via bank transfer with invoice references, ensuring full traceability and compliance with commercial standards.
5
Delivery — We coordinate vehicle transport with trusted logistics partners, with transparent communication at every step until delivery.
