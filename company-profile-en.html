About Us

Charwa GmbH is a Berlin-based commercial vehicle trading company specializing in sourcing premium used trucks and semi-trailers from across the European Union for export markets. We combine deep automotive industry expertise with proprietary technology to deliver a fast, transparent, and reliable vehicle sourcing experience.

Operating with a lean, efficient structure, Charwa is led by a hands-on Managing Director supported by a network of specialized consultants — bringing together automotive expertise, cutting-edge technology, and direct access to one of the largest exporter communities in Europe.

Why Turkmenistan — A Market in Motion

Turkmenistan — a landlocked Central Asian country — has seen commercial vehicle imports surge by several hundred percent in recent years, driven by:

Belt and Road Initiative — Overland transport from China now takes just two weeks, making Turkmenistan a key transit corridor between East Asia and Europe.

Petrochemical growth — One of the world's largest natural gas reserves, with rapidly expanding processing capacity requiring heavy-duty transport.

Agricultural expansion — Government investment in greenhouses and agriculture is driving demand for refrigerated trucks to export to Uzbekistan and beyond.
